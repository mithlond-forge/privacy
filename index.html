Privacy Policy

Sudoku, published by Mithlond Forge — last updated 22 November 2026

This app collects no data about you. None at all. It has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising.

It does not even hold the Android INTERNET permission, which means it is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere. You can verify this yourself: on the Play Store listing, under “App permissions”, internet access is not among them.

What stays on your phone

The app saves the following in its own private storage, which no other app can read:

  • the puzzle you are playing: the grid, your entries, your notes, the timer;
  • your history of finished puzzles: difficulty, duration, hints used, date;
  • which daily puzzles you have completed;
  • your settings and your progress through the lessons.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Your phone’s own backup

Android’s standard backup feature is enabled for this app. If you have turned that feature on for your device, the data above may be copied to your Google Drive backup, under your Google account. This is a service Google provides to you; the developer has no access to it. You can disable it in your phone’s settings, under Google → Backup.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled entirely on your phone. No server is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere. Turning the reminder off in the app’s settings stops it.

Sharing a result

After finishing a daily puzzle you may tap “Share”. This hands a short text — the date, the difficulty and your time — to whichever app you choose. That transfer is yours, not ours: what happens next is governed by the app you picked.

Writing to us

The app's “About” screen offers “Send feedback”. This opens the email app of your choice with a message already addressed to us, which includes the Sudoku and Android version numbers to help us understand any problem. Nothing is sent until you tap “Send” yourself, and you may edit or delete those lines. If you do write, we receive your message and your email address: they are used only to reply to you, are never passed on to anyone, and you can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

Children

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to collect from a child either. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything — for example if advertising is added — this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change. The app will also tell you within its own settings.
